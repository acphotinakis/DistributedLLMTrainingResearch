\section{Research Advances \& Synthesis}
\begin{comment}
### 4. Research Advances & Synthesis (2–3 pages)
* **Comparative Literature Review**: Synthesizes and compares the **3 primary focus research papers** (e.g., *Mist*, *PGCS*, and *Megatron-LM* or *PipeDream*).
* **Evaluation Framework**: Uses a structured framework to compare how each paper addresses:
  * Parallelism strategies (Data, Tensor, Pipeline, Expert, or Hybrid).
  * Communication scheduling and computation-communication overlap.
  * Memory optimizations (sharding, activation recomputation, offloading).
  * Experimental settings, hardware platforms, and performance speedups.
### 5. Scope, Critique & Challenges (1–2 pages)
* **Technical Critique**: Provides a justified evaluation of the limitations, weaknesses, and unstated assumptions of the focus papers (e.g., sensitivity to cross-datacenter network latency, pipeline bubbles, or hardware dependence).
* **Trade-Off Analysis**: Weighs throughput and speedup against communication overhead, load imbalance, and failure recovery risks.
* **Open Challenges**: Highlights unresolved issues in distributed LLM training, such as dynamic network heterogeneity or multi-tier memory bottlenecks.
\end{comment}
